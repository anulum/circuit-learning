# Roadmap

## Current foundation

**Being qualified:**
- repository identity and legal texts;
- architecture and governance;
- native documentation;
- dedicated validation tools.

**Domain capabilities and claims:** still empty.

## Capability milestones

1. **Add the three record schemas** with golden fixtures, and name the graph artefact contract's schemas under its companion prefix with DIRECTOR-AI. Each schema enrols its full quality gates in the change that adds it.
2. **The inventory generator,** reading the operation profiles of the circuit repositories as they exist. It lists every operation as deterministic only until a model is promoted.
3. **Grader bundle specifications for the first reward families:**
   - formal and simulation checks of RTL;
   - static timing;
   - layout rule checks.

   All on open process design kits.
4. **Study templates with their deterministic references,** and the near-duplicate and poisoning checks they require.
5. **Training and promotion through DIRECTOR-AI.** This happens only when training is approved. No model is trained before that approval.

**Built with every surface:**
- strict native quality;
- documentation;
- meaningful coverage;
- the affected benchmarks.

**Needs its own evidence and approval:**
- hosted-model use;
- public activation;
- release.

No availability date is promised. No accuracy or productivity gain is promised either.
