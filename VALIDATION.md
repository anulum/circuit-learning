# Validation

This checkout's repository foundation is under qualification. Another repository's tests or coverage do not qualify it.

**Not qualified here:**
- an inventory;
- a grader bundle;
- a study;
- a model;
- a promotion;
- a rollback.

## Maintained scope

**What the Python tools and their tests are enrolled in:**
- Ruff and formatting;
- strict mypy;
- native NumPy-style documentation;
- statement and branch coverage.

**The coverage target:** full meaningful coverage of every maintained source file.

**What the tests cover:** each repository control has a public entry point, with success and refusal tests.

## Local and CI entry points

**The environment:** the selected interpreter and the hash locks in [Contributing](CONTRIBUTING.md).

**What the named make targets inspect:** their declared scopes only.

**Full runs:** full `make ci` and `make preflight` belong in CI or in an admitted pre-push environment.

**CI for this public repository:** it runs on public material only.

## Evidence limits

**A study result's scope.** It holds only for:
- its sealed items;
- its declared reference;
- its statistical rule.

**A public benchmark result:** it is a secondary comparison, with its contamination caveat.

**A static workflow check:** it is not hosted execution.

**The maintained repository controls do not establish:**
- a model's quality;
- a model's safety;
- a release;
- a DOI;
- hosted acceptance.
